# UNINTENDED — MVP Content Depth Model

## Purpose

The MVP should become deeper before it becomes geographically larger. Content is measured by meaningful states, interpretations and consequences, not quest count.

## Required authored variation

- 6 Map cultures.
- 4 authored versions per culture.
- 24 distinct MVP Map identities.
- 3 apparent state bands per version: STABLE, FRAYED, ACTIVE.
- 72 meaningful Map presentations without requiring 72 separate physical layouts.

Four versions per culture is the MVP target. Fewer than four makes a culture too easy to reduce to a solved template. More than four before the existing versions have deep NPC knowledge, object interactions and consequences spreads authorship too thin.

## Cross-cultural echo families

The four version positions are not isolated variants. Each participates in one hidden cross-cultural echo family:

1. FIRST-CORRECTION
2. MISSING-WITNESS
3. BORROWED-AUTHORITY
4. UNFINISHED-RETURN

Each of the six cultures expresses the same hidden disturbance differently. Players are never given the internal echo identifiers. They reconstruct the relationship by travelling, comparing records, testing rules and sharing evidence.

## No fixed campaign length

Map progression is not `Map 1 -> Map 2 -> Map 3 -> final Map`.

A local Map can establish that a contradiction exists. Another Map may establish where the rule came from, whether another culture interprets it differently, or whether the contradiction belongs to a larger pattern. The useful amount of travel therefore depends on the player's theory and evidence, not a prescribed count.

A player can revisit an old Map after learning something elsewhere and discover that the old evidence now means something different.

## Two valid outward paths

Every Map version has at least two outward hooks:

- Institutional / directed: an NPC, record, dispute or unresolved matter makes another culture relevant.
- Curiosity / fooling around: unusual commands, Server probes, object handling, route experiments or anomalies expose a connection without a quest giver.

Both are first-class play. Neither is treated as the secret correct route.

## Multiple approaches, not multiple-choice solutions

Each version is authored with at least three plausible approaches and at least three possible consequences. These are server-side design truth, not a displayed solution list.

The player may resolve part of a contradiction through records, physical state, witnesses, trade, ownership, travel, institutional behaviour or retained exceptions. A Map does not need one canonical completion state.

## Anti-farming law

Repeated action in the same context rapidly loses understanding value. New context remains useful. Repeated anomaly circumstances also diminish sharply.

Locations must not become known proficiency or anomaly farms. The strongest progression pressure should come from encountering rules in new contexts and comparing behaviour across Maps.

The game should reward:

- a familiar action in an unfamiliar rule context;
- old evidence becoming newly relevant elsewhere;
- contradictory observations between Maps;
- consequences produced by player interference;
- social comparison between players with different local truths.

It should not reward:

- repeating LOOK in one room;
- repeating the same anomaly setup;
- cycling an NPC repeatable indefinitely;
- repeatedly taking replenishable evidence solely for progression;
- discovering a single optimal Map and remaining there forever.

## Content expansion gate

Do not add a fifth version to a culture until the current four have, in playable form:

- distinct NPC testimony;
- context-sensitive evidence;
- at least two meaningful interference routes;
- persistent consequences;
- at least one outward cross-Map lead;
- at least one reason to reinterpret the Map after later travel.

The same gate applies before expanding beyond the current Map count.

Depth first. Geography multiplies whatever quality already exists, including bad quality.
